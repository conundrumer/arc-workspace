## Terms

- `position`: in the feasible read, the reader's count of atoms over the form. A second branch's first atom is not a first atom here.
- `position_in_body`, and `position` in the unread read: the census's `k`, which starts again at each body and branch.
- Feasible class `own`: gold's relation is in the form's earlier atoms, whatever its kind. So a repeat of a derived form is in `own`, and the unread read counts it as derived. `derived with own` is the cell to set against the unread read's derived picks.
- `known_at_pick`: 1 where the cell is known before the pick. A class cell is known only after gold is drawn. An escape weight or a w of such a cell is no prior, so read it as an upper bound.
- `thin`: under 50 picks or under 8 tasks.
- A cell differs from the pool where the pooled figure is outside the cell's 95% task interval.
- `saved_in_coin`, feasible read: the saving inside the sampler's join coin, as the script's docstring says. The escape savings compare with `q_bits`, the share of one arm of the coin. The census adds the rest of the coin to each atom as `join mix`. The coin acts on an atom, and this figure applies it to each pick.
