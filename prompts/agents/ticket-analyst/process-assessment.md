# Process assessment
- Assess the agents' processes as a whole against [Process notes](prompts/agents/notes/process.md), for work that two processes duplicate, an outcome that no owner meets, rules that conflict, a step whose cost is out of proportion to what it finds, and a step or a rule that serves no outcome.
- Where those notes hold no map of the rules to their outcomes, map each rule of the documents under [notes](prompts/agents/notes) and of the agent definitions to an outcome of those notes, and file a `change` that adds that map to them. Where they hold one, map again each rule and each outcome that a change has altered since that map was last updated, and file a `change` that updates it.
- Settle each finding as a fault you notice, by [Settling](prompts/agents/ticket-analyst.md#settling), other than a cut of a check or a rule, which you make in no resolution: bring each such cut to the user as a decision, with the check's cost.
