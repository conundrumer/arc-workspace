# Settling tickets
- For each ticket your brief has you settle only where the session's line of work needs it, judge whether a change of that line made what the ticket records wrong or worse, or a ticket of that line waits on it. Where it does, log `own: <judgment>` on it with no transition, and settle it. Otherwise log `not own: <judgment>` on it with no transition, and leave it untaken.
- Find a ticket's duplicates under `tickets/` by their evidence, and a `request`'s by the tickets that ask for the same change.
- Check a ticket's evidence against the current tree. A `request`'s evidence is its Goal, its spans and its Grounds, in the transcript brief your brief gives, or else in the output of `uv run python scripts/spans.py brief <number>`, and, where a message of the user in a span answers a message of the coordinator, the first message of the coordinator that gives what it answers, wherever it lies before that span's end.
    - While you work on a `request`, read no entry of its session's transcript after the end of its last span.
- Where the log of a `request` you take shows a `change` filed for it before, judge its resolution again with its spans and Grounds and the part of its need that a line of its log gives as unmet.
    - Where that `change` stands under `tickets/closed/`, resolve the part of the need it leaves unmet by [Settling](prompts/agents/ticket-analyst.md#settling).
    - Otherwise, where that changes its resolution, revise that `change` where it stands under `tickets/draft/` or `tickets/open/`, with its transition to `draft` from `open`, and where it stands in another status, give the new resolution in your reply as unfinished, with its reason. Move the request back to `tickets/waiting/` as well, with a note that opens with `change` and that change's number.
- Where the last line of a `validation` ticket's log that moves it out of `tickets/deferred/` gives `exercised:`, judge its `Validation` against the runs that line gives, and where the check holds, close it with a note that opens `fixed: validated:`.
    - Otherwise settle the part of the need of the `request` its `validates` field gives that the check shows unmet, with that request's Goal, spans and Grounds as its evidence, and an `unexercised:` ticket as a fault whose root cause is why no run matched. A `change` you file for it settles the `validation` ticket, and not the request.
- Where the log of a `request` you take gives a commit of `master` that the coordinator merged for it with no `change` ticket, judge that commit as the design of a draft `change` for that request, as [Revising a draft](prompts/agents/ticket-analyst/drafts.md) gives for a draft, with the need that the request's spans and Grounds give, the tickets that log line gives the commit settles, the tickets of the earlier fixes that [Settling](prompts/agents/ticket-analyst.md#settling) reads, and the part of the need that a line of its log gives as unmet.
    - Where that commit's diff changes the agent-facing documents and no line of the request's log gives a doc reviewer's agent id for that commit, launch one `doc-reviewer` on that commit, whatever your judgment of it, and write a line in the request's log that gives the commit's hash and that doc reviewer's agent id. Resolve the request only once its log holds that line.
    - Where that commit stands as the least change that meets that need, close the request `fixed` with the commit's hash. Otherwise move the request to `tickets/waiting/` with a note that opens with `change` and the number of the `change` that takes what the commit leaves: one among the tickets that log line gives, or else one you file for it as [Resolving](prompts/agents/ticket-analyst.md#resolving) gives.
    - For each `change` outside `tickets/closed/` among the tickets that log line gives, close it `fixed` with the commit's hash where the commit meets its need, and otherwise revise it to the part of its need the commit leaves, as a `change` filed for a request before is revised, and under `tickets/assigned/` as well, with its transition to `draft` from `assigned`.
- Judge a ticket a solver pass filed with the review of that pass's transcript that your brief gives, or, where it gives none, with that transcript, read call by call, never from the pass's report alone.
- A ticket of kind `refactor` is a defect place a code reviewer or a lib reviewer found. Leave each one whose title ends in `many ideas in one place` under `tickets/open/`, untaken, and put the places of all the other `refactor` tickets your brief gives into one `change` ticket.
- Where you settle a design reviewer's `question` that gives an open `refactor` ticket by an edit in its change or by a new `change` that removes the defect that ticket records, move that `refactor` ticket to `tickets/waiting/` with a note that opens with `change` and that change's number.
