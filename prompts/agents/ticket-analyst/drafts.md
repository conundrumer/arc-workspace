# Revising a draft
- Of a draft's `Grounds`, read again only each source that has changed since: each file that `git diff <hash>` in the main tree lists, with the hash they give, each ticket they quote whose log has a line later than the draft's last `filed` or `revised` line, and each source a later line of the draft's log gives as changed.
- Settle, from the need of the tickets a draft's Motivation gives and with the findings its log gives after its start as evidence, whether a change is needed at all, and which is the least change that meets that need, with the draft's design as a default, each finding as a reason against it, and each suggestion its log gives after its start as an option against it, which you take or leave. A draft's start is the later of its last `filed` or `revised` line and the last line of its log that moves it out of `tickets/draft/`. Its resolution revises the draft, as [Change tickets](prompts/agents/notes/tickets.md#change-tickets) gives, or closes each ticket it settles, `wontfix` with the loss of its need left unmet, and then the draft, `wontfix`, or brings the user a decision, as [Resolving](prompts/agents/notes/decisions.md#resolving) gives, with the draft as its ticket.
    + xref: prompts/agents/notes/tickets.md#change-tickets, prompts/agents/notes/decisions.md#resolving
    - Where the operator's line moved the draft past its cost budget since its start, narrow it to the work its worktree meets, or, where it meets none, to a part of its work that its editors can finish within that budget, and file the rest, in parts of that size, each as a `change` that gives the draft in its `depends-on`.
    - Where you leave every suggestion and the log gives no finding after its start, move the draft to `tickets/approved/` with no revision, with a note that opens with `suggestion left`.
    - Cut each line of the draft that a finding falls on and that [An edit's items](prompts/agents/notes/launching.md#an-edits-items) keeps out of an Intent.
        + xref: prompts/agents/notes/launching.md#an-edits-items
    - Where a finding is a defensible reason against a mechanism the design relies on, take it as the strongest reason against that mechanism, drop that mechanism and reframe the problem, as [How a decision is made](prompts/agents/notes/decisions.md#how-a-decision-is-made) gives for a default, in place of a revision of that mechanism for that case.
        + xref: prompts/agents/notes/decisions.md#how-a-decision-is-made

    - Findings of two ticket reviewers on one mechanism of the design are such a reason. The findings on a mechanism before a reframe count where the reframe keeps or brings back that mechanism, and a mechanism the reframe brings anew has none. A finding on a claim about the current state, a check the Scope gives or the order of changes falls on no mechanism.
- Think a revised design through whole, with each mechanism the revision adds, for how it can fail.
