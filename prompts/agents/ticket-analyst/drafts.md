# Revising a draft
- Read the draft's `Grounds` before you explore, and read again only each source of them that has changed since: each file that `git diff <hash>` in the main tree lists, with the hash they give, each ticket they quote whose log has a line later than the draft's last `filed` or `revised` line, and each source a later line of the draft's log gives as changed.
- Then settle, from the need of the tickets its Motivation gives and with the findings its log gives as evidence, whether a change is needed at all, and which is the least change that meets that need, with the draft's design as a default, each finding as a reason against it, and each suggestion its log gives as an option against it, which you take or leave. Then revise the draft, as [Change tickets](prompts/agents/notes/tickets.md#change-tickets) gives, or close each ticket it settles, `wontfix` with the loss of its need left unmet, and then the draft, `wontfix`, or bring the user a decision, as [Resolving](prompts/agents/ticket-analyst.md#resolving) gives, with the draft as its ticket.
    - Where you leave every suggestion and the log gives no finding after its last `filed` or `revised` line, move the draft to `tickets/open/` with no revision, with a note that opens with `suggestion left`.
    - Cut each line of the draft that a finding falls on and that [Launching](prompts/agents/notes/launching.md#launching) keeps out of an Intent.
    - Where a finding is a defensible reason against a mechanism the design relies on, take it as the strongest reason against that mechanism, drop that mechanism and reframe the problem, as [How a decision is made](prompts/agents/notes/decisions.md#how-a-decision-is-made) gives for a default, in place of a revision of that mechanism for that case.
        * comment: With the default line of [How a decision is made](prompts/agents/notes/decisions.md#how-a-decision-is-made) in its context, ticket analyst a1d003e96bb28badf kept the mechanisms of draft change 2-487 after repeated findings against them, without a reframe: the Motivation of ticket 2-495.
    - Findings of two ticket reviewers on one mechanism of the design are such a reason. The findings on a mechanism before a reframe count where the reframe keeps or brings back that mechanism, and a mechanism the reframe brings anew has none. A finding on a claim about the current state, a check the Scope gives or the order of changes falls on no mechanism.
    - Then think the whole revised design through, with each mechanism the revision adds, for how it can fail.
