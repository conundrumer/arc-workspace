Its witness `shape-margin-two` shows it defined and differing from the concept, and the audit's witness and dropped-case checks pass.

- New witnesses `shape-margin-two` for `$GapFromShapeMargin`, and `shape-panel-right` and `field-panel-dominant` for pairs of cases that no run separated (create). Judgments gives the reason for each.

## Judgments
- The `@ignore: case section` line above `.init copies` is removed: the run's lint reported that no finding or hint with that category fires on `copies`. With the line gone, lint is ok.
- The other Not formed items of the Analyze report give no case. A stored repetition axis fails Fit (train0 repeats down, the other pairs across). Alternating a D4 transform fails Fit at train2, whose asymmetric shape repeats unchanged. A gap in the fill color, or touching copies, fails Fit at every pair. N as the number of mark lines fails Fit at train2 (five columns, four copies).
- Panels: `DividerTiles` over the full lines of `divider_color`. The panels are ordered toward each end of the panel axis by an `Order` over tile positions, using `nearer`. The roles come from `first`, `next` and `last` rather than from tile indices. `part_idx` counts divider lines, so with thick dividers the indices are not dense, and with `Order` the concept still gives its output on `thick-dividers`, as the Analyze file gives (create).
- `$MarkCells`: the mark cells set the extent of an `AxisFrame`, and `divide_by_size` by `mark_size` with gap 0 cuts that frame. The outer frame's extent is the copy count. On a cell count that is not a multiple of `mark_size`, nothing decomposes, and the case is undefined (create).
- `$GapFromMarkSpacing`: a `Ray` is cast from each mark cell along the four cardinals, and every cell off `background` blocks it. A cast counts when its `stop` cell is a mark of another component under the case's mark reading (4-connected for `$Marks4` and `$MarkCells`, 8-connected for `$Marks8`). The gap is the least background count of such a cast (create).
- `$GapFromDivider`: each divider band (`Components` over divider-line cells) is counted in lines, and the counts must agree (`Unanimous`) (create).
- The output: `concat_slab` places each copy index and each box-relative cell, with the case's gap. A `Frame2D` over that extent gives the frame, and every frame cell that is not a shape copy takes the field color (create).
- `shape-margin-two`, `variant concept`: the shape sits two cells in from every side, and that breaks no scene claim. Panel sizes vary across the pairs, and a variant task could routinely give a larger margin. The verdict follows the Analyze report's reading that the gap is a fixed one line (create).
- `shape-panel-right`, `variant concept`: the left-right mirror of train3. Without it, no run showed `$RolesByContent` defined and differing from `$PanelOrder` under `$PanelInset` and under `$GapFromShapeMargin`. On it, `$PanelOrder` reads from the top-left, takes the solid left panel as the shape panel, finds no mark in the next panel, and is undefined. It stands at the stored assignment, because no `start_corner` makes `$PanelOrder` defined and different there (create).
- `background=DominantColor`, `variant derived`, `@relaxed shape-panel` and `count-panel`: a recolored background is a color-permutation variant. It breaks the scene claims' "background 0" (create).
- `field-panel-dominant`, `ordinary concept`, a second witness of the `background` point, with no `@witness` line: a 10-wide field panel makes 8 the commonest color, and the derived background then finds no background run between the marks. Panel widths vary across the pairs, as in test1, and this breaks no scene claim. Without it, no run showed `background=stored` defined and differing (create).
- Hints: the `single-consumer` hint on `count_panel` was cleared by inlining. The `common-subexpr` hint in Determine was cleared by `line_cell`. The `choice-tag` finding on `mark` was cleared with one clause that excludes `$Marks8()` (create).
- Markers: `marks4` stands under `$Marks4, $MarkCells & $GapFromMarkSpacing`, because `$MarkCells` reads 4-connected marks only for the mark-spacing gap (create).
- Deletions: the survey gives the same four as before. `mark($Marks8(), ...)` and the `facing.stop` atom change nothing on the pairs, because the pairs never instantiate them: no marks touch at a corner, and every counted run ends on a mark. Both are kept. The `ex_out` atoms in `lacks` and `margin` restrict the determination to train examples, as [Determination](docs/handbook/solver-file.md#determination) gives, and are kept (create).
- Determination: the plan was over the enumeration limit. The Determine comp derives `divider_color`, the color whose every cell lies on a one-colored line of that color in every train input. Color 0 also forms full lines, so "a one-colored line" alone did not determine it. It also derives `gap_width`, from the runs of output lines all in the field color, and `margin`, from the shape's distance from its panel's sides under `$RolesByContent`. The runner covers the rest (create).
- Simplify and Prune: the code states each step with lib relations and no needless indirection, and the comments say only what the names do not. Nothing else changed.
- Keywords: `decompose tile-arrangement crop bbox objects count mapping`. `mapping` is for the panel colors that the shape color and the field take (create).
- `src/` was grepped once, for the category string to write in `@ignore`. The hint text already prints it, so the grep was not needed (create).
