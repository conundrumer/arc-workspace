# f8f52ecc progress, refine-1

- interference: `uv run python scripts/run_dl.py f8f52ecc` timed out (20 s limit) on train 2 and test 1 after `$TurnsFirst` was added. Cause: Souffle does not use an index for an atom argument written as a record term, even where every field is bound; it scans the relation and unpacks each row (`--show=transformed-ram` shows `FOR t IN run.edge` with no `ON INDEX` for `run.edge([Ch, Col, S0], S, S2)`). A keyed lib comp read with a key built in the clause from `Ch`, `Col` and another variable is therefore scanned once per outer row. The fix was to order the atoms so that the first atom binds a whole record that a later atom takes in a column of its own (`run.reached([Ch, Col, S0], S)` first, then `bends.distance(..., S0, _)` and `run.edge(..., S, S2)`), and to read the arrivals through a flat helper relation. Cost: about 15 minutes of profiling by hand (`souffle -p`, `souffleprof`). The output was not wrong; the runs now take about 4 s in all. No handbook line says that atom order or record-term keys decide the speed of a lookup.
