## What I see

The task: the 5x5 input becomes the top-left quarter of a 10x10 output. Outside it, the main diagonal continues with period 3, and each new diagonal cell paints an arm up its column and an arm left along its row, one shell out. The case has two sides: DETs that read the train pairs (&r7 to &r12, &r15 to &r33), and RULEs that apply what they find (&r1 to &r3, &r13, &r14, &r23, &r34 to &r36). Every table has one assignment. The DET tables are task-wide, with an Example column.
